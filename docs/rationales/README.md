# Rationales Index

Navigation from each normative owner to its WHY document. Rationales explain
why the contracts in `docs/` were chosen, including the incidents and
alternatives behind them; they do not define schemas or behavior.

- `documentation-authority.md` — why this repository splits WHAT (specs),
  WHY (rationales), and HOW (scripts/templates), and why protocol rules no
  longer carry their incident history inline.
- `measurement-protocol.md` — the incident behind each measurement rule.
- `evidence-records.md` — why the ledgers have a controlled outcome
  vocabulary, single owners, and identity tuples.
